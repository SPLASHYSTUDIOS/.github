# Splashy Studios

**Splashy Studios** builds software for small service businesses. We're the team behind **The Splash Club** and **SplashyPages** — helping local businesses get found online, generate leads, and grow.

---

## What We Build

### **The Splash Club**
A done-for-you local SEO agency for small service businesses — plumbers, roofers, HVAC techs, electricians, landscapers, movers, cleaners, painters, contractors, and dentists. We handle everything from website creation to Google Maps optimization, generating 15–40 qualified leads per month for our clients.

Website: [thesplashclub.com](https://www.thesplashclub.com)

### **SplashyPages**
A self-serve SaaS website builder and local SEO toolkit purpose-built for small businesses. Sign up, chat with Splashbot (our AI onboarding assistant), and get a fully optimized multi-page website in minutes.

Features include an AI customer chatbot, lead management inbox, analytics dashboard, Scout SEO heatmap rank tracker, citation tracking across 30+ directories, AI-powered blog tools, and actionable SEO insights.

Website: [thesplashclub.com/pages](https://www.thesplashclub.com/pages)

### **splashy-video**
Programmatic video production pipeline built with Remotion. Creates marketing videos, product walkthroughs, and social content for our products.
